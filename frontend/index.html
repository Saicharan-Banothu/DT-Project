Audio Recording-Bias Audit

Raw audio datasets often carry recording biases (sample rates, microphone formats). Preprocessing all audio with a standardized Telephony Profile (16 kHz mono, 300–3400 Hz bandpass, -20 dBFS loudness norm) reduced dumb baseline accuracy from 72.44% to 59.56%, ensuring the classifier relies on genuine vocal tension and temporal rhythm.
