Simple Technology

How SentiCall Keeps You Safe

Built on Design Thinking principles to provide warmth, reassurance, and crystal-clear clarity.

1

Listens to Voice Tone

Evaluates speech cadence, tension, tempo, and digital VoIP background patterns characteristic of robocall centers.

2

Transcribes & Reads

Converts speech to text on your local machine with OpenAI Whisper, ensuring your privacy is completely respected.

3

Unmasks Scam Tactics

Detects authority impersonation, false deadlines, OTP demands, and prize lures, highlighting exact words in plain English.
